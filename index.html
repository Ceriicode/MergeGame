How to play

✨

Summon

Spend gold to call a random hero from your deck onto a free tile. Each summon costs a little more.

⭐

Merge

Drag a hero onto a matching one with the same stars. They fuse into a stronger hero, up to ★5.

🛡️

Hold the lanes

Enemies march down 5 lanes. Fighters strike what passes their tile, archers and mages shoot nearby lanes. Drag heroes to move them.

💎

Grow stronger

Win gems to unlock and level heroes, pick your deck of 5, and buy upgrades that last forever.
